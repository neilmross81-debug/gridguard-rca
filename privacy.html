Privacy Policy — TotalRCA

Effective Date: 19 November 2026 · Contact: neil.m.ross81@gmail.com

TotalRCA is a local-first Root Cause Analysis (RCA) and Corrective & Preventive Action (CAPA) application designed for offline individuals and engineering teams.

1. Data Storage (Local-First by Default)

By default, all investigations, 5 Whys trees, Ishikawa 6M diagrams, FMEA risk matrices, CAPA actions, timelines, and attachments you create in TotalRCA are stored locally on your device. TotalRCA does not operate a central database and does not collect, transmit, or sell your investigation data.

2. Optional Team Database Sync (Bring Your Own Database)

If you choose to upgrade to TotalRCA Pro and configure your own external sync destination in Settings (such as your own Supabase project, Google Apps Script web app, or custom REST endpoint), your data is transmitted directly from your device to the endpoint you specify. You are solely responsible for the security, access controls, and privacy practices of your own database or endpoint.

3. In-App Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time Pro purchases are processed securely by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play) via RevenueCat. We do not receive or store your payment card details. RevenueCat processes an anonymous app user ID and purchase receipt solely to verify and restore your Pro entitlement across your devices.

4. Analytics & Tracking

TotalRCA does not use third-party advertising SDKs, behavioral tracking, or cross-app tracking.

5. Data Deletion

You can delete all local data at any time from within the app's Settings ("Reset to sample data") or by uninstalling the app from your device.

6. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact neil.m.ross81@gmail.com.
